Privacy Policy — NABL LIMS Dashboard

Last updated: September 2026

This application ("NABL LIMS Dashboard") is an internal laboratory information management tool used by a coal testing laboratory to track equipment, training, documents, and quality records required for ISO/IEC 17025:2017 / NABL accreditation.

What data this app accesses

When a user connects their Google account, this app requests access only to files and folders that this app itself creates in the user's Google Drive (Google's drive.file scope). It cannot see, read, or modify any other files in the user's Drive.

How data is stored

All laboratory records entered into the dashboard (equipment, training, documents, staff, and related quality records) are saved directly into a Google Drive folder owned by the signed-in user's own Google account. No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by the developer. There is no backend server — the application runs entirely in the user's browser and communicates directly with Google's own APIs.

Data sharing

This application does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. Data is only ever exchanged between the user's browser and the user's own Google Drive account.

Who can access this app

This application is used internally by a specific laboratory's staff and is not intended for general public use.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the laboratory's Quality Manager.
